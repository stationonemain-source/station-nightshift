What DORIAN POOLS & CONSTRUCTION does

DORIAN POOLS & CONSTRUCTION is a design-build studio: one team from the first sketch to the first swim. We design the pool, engineer it, and build it ourselves — no subbed-out shells, no handoffs between trades, no surprises halfway through the season. That means custom gunite pools and spas, water and fire features, the outdoor room around them, and renovations of pools worth saving. What you get is quiet geometry, honest stone, and water engineered to hold still.

The build

Fourteen weeks, five chapters.

  1. Design & engineeringWeeks 1–3

    3D design in your actual backyard, geotech borings, structural engineering, HOA & permits.

  2. Excavation & steelWeeks 4–5

    Dig, plumb and a steel cage tied by hand — the skeleton every quiet surface hangs on.

  3. Gunite shellWeek 6

    Eleven inches of pneumatically-placed concrete, then a supervised 28-day wet cure.

  4. Stone, tile & deckingWeeks 7–11

    Coping, waterline tile, decking and the outdoor room — the slow, visible craft.

  5. Interior & first fillWeeks 12–14

    Interior finish, water chemistry start-up, equipment orientation. Then we hand you the calm.
