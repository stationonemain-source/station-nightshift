How the check works

It reads your site twice: once as a visitor, once as Google.

  1. We read your public pagesYour home page and a sample of your other public pages, one at a time, the way a visitor's browser loads them.
  2. Then we read them as GoogleAs a visitor arriving from a Google search, and as Google's own crawler. Some hacks only show themselves to Google, so this is where they turn up.
  3. We look for links that hideLinks pushed off-screen, shrunk to nothing or shown only to Google, pointing to unrelated sites such as online drug shops, wagering sites or adult sites.
  4. We only say "found" with proof"Hidden links found" needs a screenshot, the hidden code itself and where the link goes. Without all three, we don't say it.
  5. Unclear? A person looksIf something looks off but isn't clear-cut, a person at Station checks it and emails you the answer, usually within one business day.
  6. You get the resultOn screen, usually in under a minute, and by email. Your result has its own link and an 8-character code, so you can come back to it.

What it can't see. The check reads public pages only. It never logs in, never tries passwords and never changes anything. It can't see files that nothing links to, or anything inside your hosting account, so "Nothing found" means nothing was visible from outside on the day we checked. It is not a security certificate. How the cleanup works

A backup first, a person in the loop, and 30 days of watching.

Before any work, you accept a short cleanup authorization. You confirm you own or control the site, you bring in your web developer (whoever built it or looks after it), and you keep your own backup as well as ours.

  1. Back it upA full copy of your files and database before anything changes. Ask, and we put it back.
  2. Check every fileCore, plugin and theme files are compared with the official copies. Anything different is listed.
  3. Replace what was changedAltered files are swapped for clean copies of the same version.
  4. Clean the databaseInjected links and hidden blocks are found, and so are the ways back in, such as unknown admin users and back doors.
  5. A person approves the listNothing is deleted until someone at Station has reviewed exactly what would go.
  6. Harden it and prove itThe site is hardened, then the same check runs again, as a visitor and as Google, and must come back with nothing found.

Then we watch it. The site is checked daily for 30 days, and if the same kind of hidden links come back in that time, we clean them again at no charge. That doesn't cover a new problem, such as one caused by a reused password. Because Station didn't build your site or look after it, our responsibility for the site is to restore the backup we took, and it never goes beyond what you paid. We can't promise how search engines will treat your site afterwards. Read the cleanup authorization

Questions

Asked straight, answered straight.

Is this the same as being hacked?

Yes. Someone changed your site without your permission. Usually nothing is stolen: your site is used to point search engines at someone else's websites, and Google reads those links even though you and your customers never see them.

Do you need my login to check my site?

No. The check reads only the public pages that a browser or Google can already read. It never logs in, never tries passwords and never changes anything.

Why do you ask for my email?

So the result reaches the right person. We email it to the address you type in, and we don't send you marketing unless you ask for it. Our privacy policy has the details.

What happens if nothing is found?

The result says so, plainly, and there's nothing to buy. If you'd like, Station also runs a free front-office audit and a free website demo.

Could you say my site was hacked when it wasn't?

We work hard not to. "Hidden links found" is only shown with proof you can check yourself: a screenshot, the hidden code and where it points. Anything unclear goes to a person first.

My site isn't on WordPress. Can you clean it?

For now, cleanups are for WordPress sites. For anything else, such as Wix, Squarespace, Shopify or a custom build, the check and the full report work the same way, and we'll tell you whether a cleanup is possible and quote it.

What do you need from me for a cleanup?

You accept the cleanup authorization, then install a small connector plugin on your WordPress site and paste its key into our form. The key is stored encrypted, used only by the cleanup, and deleted 7 days after the job closes.

I got an email from Station about my website. Is it real?

Our emails come from Station Team <hello@send.station.solutions>. The only links in them are your result at audit.station.solutions/sweep and our unsubscribe page on station.solutions. You never need to click: type station.solutions/sweep into your browser and enter the code from the email. We never ask for a password by email, nothing needs to be bought, and you don't need to reply.

How do I stop your emails?

Use the unsubscribe link at the bottom of any email from us, or reply "unsubscribe". Either one stops all email from Station to that address.
