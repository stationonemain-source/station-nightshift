Part 2 — Terms

1. The Program and Your Role

1.1 What the Program is. Station runs an invitation-only partner program (the “Program”). As a Partner you introduce Station’s products to businesses, close sales, and manage the ongoing relationship with the clients in your Book of Business (Section 6) — mainly upsell conversations and general account questions. Station pays you the commission in Section 7 on the revenue those clients actually pay.

1.2 Invitation only. Partner status is extended by Station, in its sole discretion, after a direct conversation. There is no application form, waitlist, or automatic path to it.

1.3 One track. This Agreement describes the only participation track Station currently offers. Station does not currently enroll referral-only participants, and nothing in this Agreement gives you a right to any other track, bounty, bonus, or payment that is not written here. If Station later offers another track, it will be under separate written terms.

1.4 What this replaces. This Agreement replaces every earlier partner or affiliate terms between you and Station regarding the Program, signed or not, including the “Station Affiliate Program Agreement” (v4 and v4.1), and its Scout and Partner tracks.

2. Eligibility and Getting Started

2.1 You must be at least 18 years old and legally able to enter this Agreement. If you sign for an entity, you confirm you have authority to bind it, and the entity and you both accept this Agreement.

2.2 You will give Station accurate information and keep it current (name, address, email, payout details, tax status).

2.3 Tax form first. No payout of any kind is made until your tax documentation is on file: an IRS Form W-9 for U.S. persons, or an IRS Form W-8BEN for non-U.S. individuals. Commission may accrue before the form is on file, but nothing is paid until it is.

2.4 Station may decline, suspend, or remove any Partner, including for spam, misrepresentation, fraudulent referrals, breach of this Agreement, or conduct that damages Station’s brand. Station may decline participants from any jurisdiction where participation would be unlawful or impractical.

3. What Station Provides

3.1 Station will provide you with: (a) a unique referral link and code, and tracking of visits and leads recorded to your code; (b) access to the Partner portal, which includes your book of business, lead pipeline, demo builder, call sheet, booking calendar, training, and products & pricing; (c) Station’s current published pricing and approved marketing materials; (d) notifications about bookings and purchases by your clients; (e) technical delivery, onboarding, support, billing, and collections for every client; and (f) statements and payouts under Sections 7 and 8.

3.2 You are not responsible for building, fixing, or hosting anything a client buys. That work belongs to Station.

3.3 Station alone decides: which products it offers and at what price; any discount, credit, free trial, or refund; whether to accept, pause, or end any client; the terms clients sign; and how Station’s services are delivered. Station may change, suspend, or end any tool, material, or feature at any time. The portal and tools are provided as they are, and Station does not promise they will always be available.

4. What You Do

4.1 You will promote and sell Station honestly, follow Section 11, and use only Station’s published pricing and terms.

4.2 For each client in your Book of Business you are the client’s first point of contact for (a) upsell and expansion conversations and (b) general account questions that are not technical break/fix. You will respond reasonably promptly (Station’s expectation is within two business days), pass technical problems to Station’s support quickly with the client’s details, tell Station promptly if a client wants to cancel, complains, or raises anything that could involve a refund, dispute, or legal issue, and keep material notes and lead status current in the portal.

4.3 You will not promise a client custom work, features, timelines, credits, or discounts that Station has not approved in writing.

4.4 You pay your own costs (equipment, software, travel, marketing spend, insurance, taxes). Station does not reimburse them.

4.5 No quota, no exclusivity. Station sets no sales minimum and does not promise you any volume of leads. You may do other work, but not by using Station’s confidential information, client data, or leads, and subject to Section 14.

5. Referrals, Attribution and Free Trials

5.1 A referral is credited to you when a prospect arrives through your link, or is otherwise recorded to your code in Station’s systems, including a lead you enter in the portal and Station accepts.

5.2 Station’s tracking records control. If more than one Partner claims the same prospect, Station decides.

5.3 Referrals must be genuine, arm’s-length businesses. Self-referrals, fake or shell clients, and any business that you, your family, or your entity’s owners own or control are not eligible for commission. Violations are grounds for termination for cause under Section 15.4.

5.4 Free trials. Station may offer a new client a free trial (7, 14, or 30 days), decided case by case at Station’s sole discretion. No commission is earned on a client who is on a free trial. Commission begins only when the trial has ended and the client’s first payment has actually cleared.

6. Leads and Your Book of Business

6.1 Lead assignment. Station may, but need not, assign inbound leads and existing clients to you. Station assigns manually, at its sole discretion (workload, fit, or any other basis), and may change how it assigns at any time. Station does not promise any number of leads.

6.2 Book of Business. A client joins your “Book of Business” when (a) you personally close the client and Station accepts the order, or (b) Station assigns a lead or client to you and that client becomes a paying client. Station records the assignment in the portal.

6.3 Operational assignment, not ownership. Every client contracts directly with Station, is billed by Station, and remains Station’s client. Station keeps full and exclusive ownership of the client relationship, the client contract, and all client data. Nothing in this Agreement gives you any legal, equitable, or proprietary interest in any client, in Station’s business, or in the Book of Business itself. The Book of Business only records who is responsible for servicing an account and who is paid commission on it.

6.4 One client, one Partner. Each client is assigned to exactly one Partner at a time. There is no override on other Partners’ clients, no stacking, and no multi-level component of any kind.

6.5 Reassignment. Station may reassign a client to another Partner or handle it in-house: (a) at the client’s request; (b) if Station reasonably concludes you are not meeting Section 4.2, after giving you notice and a reasonable chance to fix it; (c) if you breach this Agreement; or (d) when this Agreement ends (Section 15). Commission on a reassigned client stops for months after the reassignment date; amounts already earned stay yours, subject to Sections 8 and 9.

6.6 Starting Book. Clients already assigned to you on the Effective Date are listed in the Partner Schedule. If none is listed, none is assigned. A record in the portal that is not listed and that Station has not accepted under Section 6.2 does not put a client in your Book.

7. Commission

7.1 Rate. You earn the flat commission rate stated in the Partner Schedule on Commissionable Revenue from each client in your Book of Business, for as long as (a) the client remains an active, paying Station client, (b) the client remains in your Book, and (c) this Agreement remains in effect. You earn nothing else under this Agreement: no bounty, no setup bonus, no tiers.

7.2 Commissionable Revenue means the recurring subscription fees for Station products that a client has actually paid to Station and that have cleared, after refunds, chargebacks, credits, and discounts. It does not include: (a) one-time fees, such as setup, onboarding, website build, or other single purchases; (b) sales, use, or similar taxes; (c) amounts Station charges only to recover third-party costs; or (d) anything not yet paid and cleared. Payment-processing fees are not deducted. Commission is never calculated on list price, quoted price, or projected amounts.

7.3 Prepayments. If a client prepays for more than one month, the commission is earned in equal monthly installments over the months prepaid, and paid on the normal cycle, not as a lump sum. You earn an installment only for a month in which this Agreement is in effect and the client is in your Book.

7.4 Statements. Station shows your accrued commission in the portal and on payout statements. Tell Station in writing within 60 days of a statement if you believe it is wrong, and Station will review it in good faith. Station’s payment and tracking records control.

7.5 Rate changes. The rate stays as stated in the Partner Schedule unless changed under Section 16.

8. Payouts and Taxes

8.1 Payouts are manual and run every two weeks. Each run covers commission on payments that have cleared since the previous run, cross-checked against Station’s payment-processor records before release. Station may hold commission on a payment for up to 30 days after it clears (the refund and chargeback window), as described in Section 9.2.

8.2 The payout minimum is $50 of accrued commission. Smaller balances roll forward without expiring, subject to Sections 2.3, 9, and 15.

8.3 Station pays by the method in the Partner Schedule, to the details you give. You are responsible for keeping them correct; Station is not liable for a payment sent to details you provided.

8.4 Station will issue IRS Form 1099 (or apply withholding) where the law requires. You are solely responsible for your own income taxes, self-employment taxes, and any other taxes on amounts paid to you.

8.5 Station may deduct from any payout an amount you owe Station under this Agreement (for example, a clawback under Section 9).

9. Refunds, Chargebacks and Clawbacks

9.1 If a client’s payment is refunded, charged back, or otherwise reversed, the related commission is reversed and deducted from your accrued balance or next payout. If no balance remains, Station may invoice you for the difference.

9.2 If a client cancels within 30 days after paying (after any free trial), Station may withhold the related commission until it confirms the payment has finally cleared and will not be reversed.

10. Confidentiality; Client Data and Privacy

10.1 Confidential information. Non-public information you learn about Station, its clients, its pricing internals, its tools, or how the Program works is confidential. You will use it only to perform this Agreement and will not disclose it to anyone else. This lasts for as long as the information stays confidential and survives termination.

10.2 Client and lead data. You may access client and lead contact details and account information only as needed to do the work in Section 4. You will: (a) use it only to service that client and do your Station work; (b) not sell it, share it, export it in bulk, or copy it outside Station’s systems (for example, into a personal CRM, spreadsheet, or mailing list); (c) not use it to market any other product, service, or business; (d) keep your portal login private, never share it, and use reasonable security on the devices you use; (e) follow applicable privacy law and Station’s Privacy Policy; (f) tell Station promptly, and within 72 hours, if you suspect any unauthorized access to or disclosure of it; and (g) when this Agreement ends, stop using it, delete any copy you hold, and confirm in writing if Station asks.

10.3 Your portal shows the clients in your Book and aggregate counts for your own referrals. It does not show other Partners’ clients.

11. Promotion Rules and Compliance

11.1 Disclose your connection. You must clearly and conspicuously disclose that you earn compensation from Station (for example, “I earn a commission if you sign up through my link”) in every endorsement, review, post, or recommendation, as the FTC’s Endorsement Guides (16 C.F.R. Part 255) and equivalent laws require.

11.2 No spam. You may not send unsolicited bulk email, texts, or calls to promote Station. Email must comply with CAN-SPAM. Calls and texts must comply with the TCPA and applicable state telemarketing law, including consent, do-not-call, and calling-hours rules.

11.3 Be accurate. You must describe Station and its pricing accurately and use only published prices and terms. You must not guarantee earnings or outcomes for a client or for yourself on Station’s behalf, bid on Station’s brand or trademarked terms in paid search, impersonate Station, or use deceptive or false-urgency tactics.

11.4 You may not offer rebates, kickbacks, or side incentives funded from your commission without Station’s prior written consent.

11.5 You will comply with all laws that apply to your promotion and your work under this Agreement.

12. Trademark License

12.1 Station grants you a limited, non-exclusive, non-transferable, revocable license to use Station’s name, logo, and approved marketing materials solely to promote Station under this Agreement.

12.2 Station may revoke this license at any time by notice. When it is revoked or this Agreement ends, you must promptly stop using Station’s marks and materials. All goodwill from your use belongs to Station.

13. Independent Contractor; No Authority

13.1 You are an independent contractor. Nothing in this Agreement creates an employment, agency, partnership, joint venture, or franchise relationship, and you are not an owner of Station. You decide when, where, and how you work, and you are not entitled to wages, employee benefits, or unemployment or workers’ compensation coverage from Station. Station does not withhold taxes from your commission.

13.2 You have no authority to bind Station. You may not sign contracts, quote non-published or custom pricing, accept payments, make commitments, or incur obligations on Station’s behalf, and you must not suggest that you can.

14. Non-Circumvention and Non-Solicitation

14.1 While this Agreement is in effect and for 12 months after it ends, you will not, directly or indirectly: (a) encourage or ask any client in your Book of Business, or any client you dealt with through the Program, to cancel, downgrade, or not renew Station services; (b) sell to, or refer, those clients to a product or service that competes with the Station products they use; or (c) divert to a competing provider a prospect you learned about through the Program.

14.2 This Section is limited to clients and prospects you dealt with through the Program. It does not stop you from working in your field generally, or from serving anyone who came to you independently without any use of Station’s confidential information, client data, or leads. You agree these limits are reasonable and necessary to protect Station’s clients, data, and goodwill. If a court finds any limit unenforceable, the court may narrow it to the extent enforceable, and the rest of this Section stays in effect. Station may seek an injunction to enforce this Section and Section 10 in addition to any other remedy.

15. Term and Termination

15.1 This Agreement runs from the Effective Date until terminated. Either party may terminate it at any time, with or without cause, by written notice (email suffices). Station may suspend your access immediately if it reasonably suspects a breach.

15.2 If you leave. Give Station written notice. Your clients are then reassigned under Section 6.5(d), at Station’s sole discretion, to other Partners or kept in-house. Commission for months after your termination date stops.

15.3 What you are paid. On termination Station will pay commission accrued on payments that cleared through the termination date, either once above the $50 minimum or on the final payout run, whichever Station elects, subject to Sections 2.3 and 9. There is no commission after the termination date, even on clients you brought in or managed. There is no post-termination tail.

15.4 For cause. If Station terminates for cause (fraud, fake or self-referrals, spam, misrepresentation, material breach, misuse of client data, or conduct that damages Station’s brand), Station may withhold and forfeit accrued but unpaid commission, in addition to any other remedy.

15.5 On termination you must stop using your referral link, Station’s marks, and Program materials, and comply with Section 10.2(g). Sections 7.4, 9, 10, 12.2, 13, 14, 17, and 18, and any amount owed under Section 9, survive termination.

16. Program Changes

16.1 Station may update the Program and this Agreement with 30 days’ written notice (email or portal posting suffices). If you keep participating after the notice period, you accept the update. If you do not accept it, your remedy is to terminate under Section 15.

16.2 Changes are prospective only. No change reduces commission already accrued on payments that cleared before the change took effect.

17. Disclaimers; Limitation of Liability; Indemnity

17.1 The Program is provided “as is.” Station does not guarantee any volume of leads, referrals, conversions, or earnings. Any example of earnings that Station gives you is only an illustration of arithmetic, not a forecast or promise.

17.2 To the maximum extent permitted by law, neither party is liable to the other for indirect, incidental, consequential, or punitive damages, and Station’s total liability under this Agreement is limited to the commission properly owed and unpaid to you when the claim arises. This does not limit your liability under Section 17.3 or for breach of Sections 10 or 14.

17.3 Indemnity. You will defend and indemnify Station and its owners, managers, and personnel against third-party claims, and resulting fines, penalties, and reasonable legal costs, arising from (a) your promotion or marketing, including unlawful calls, texts, or emails, false or unauthorized statements, and missing disclosures; (b) your breach of Section 10, 11, or 14; or (c) your misuse of client or lead data.

18. Governing Law; Disputes; General

18.1 Law and venue. This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Texas, without regard to conflict-of-law rules. The state or federal courts sitting in Harris County, Texas have exclusive venue for any dispute, and each party consents to that jurisdiction.

18.2 Talk first. Before starting a lawsuit, a party will send written notice of the dispute and the parties will discuss it in good faith for 30 days. This does not apply to a request for an injunction under Section 14.2.

18.3 Assignment. You may not assign this Agreement without Station’s written consent. Station may assign it to a successor of its business.

18.4 Entire agreement; other agreements. This Agreement, with its Partner Schedule, is the entire agreement about the Program and replaces prior Program terms as stated in Section 1.4. It does not change, replace, or waive any other agreement between you and Station or any affiliate of Station (for example, an agreement for services, equity, or another business), and amounts paid under one agreement are not credited against another unless that agreement says so.

18.5 Notices. Notices to you go to the email address in the Partner Schedule. Notices to Station go to main@station.solutions, with a copy by mail to Station Automations Group LLC, 1856 Branard St, Houston, TX 77098. A notice sent by email is received when sent.

18.6 General. If a provision is unenforceable, the rest stands. A delay in enforcing a right is not a waiver of it. Headings are for convenience only. No provision is construed against a party because that party drafted it. This Agreement may be signed in counterparts and electronically, and you consent to using electronic signatures and records under the federal E-SIGN Act and the Texas Uniform Electronic Transactions Act.

18.7 You have had the chance to review it. You confirm that you have read this Agreement, that Station has told you that you may have your own lawyer and tax adviser review it before you sign, and that nothing in this Agreement or in any summary Station gave you is legal or tax advice. If a summary conflicts with this Agreement, this Agreement controls.
