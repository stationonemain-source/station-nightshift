How the money works

Scout and Partner pay differently — see the Training tab for the full breakdown. Short version:

  1. Scout — flat bounty, one time. Share your link. Once someone you referred is a live, paying client (trial period over if they had one), you're paid a flat bounty for that product — $25 to $2,000 depending which one. No recurring %, but no cap on how many you stack.
  2. Partner — 40% recurring, forever. You close and manage your own clients (or ones distributed to you). You earn 40% of what they actually pay Station, every month they stay — calculated on cleared invoices, never list price. No bounty on top, no tier to climb.
  3. You get paid monthly. We need your tax form on file first (W-9 US / W-8BEN outside the US).

Annual prepayments credit to you across 12 months either way.
