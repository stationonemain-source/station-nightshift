Behind the counter

We're a two-oven bakery with a counter, a coffee machine and three tables by the window.

Sam started out selling bread at the Saturday market. Now we bake six days a week, from half past three in the morning, and close the doors when the shelves are empty. Most days that's around one o'clock.

Everything is made here, by hand, the same day. Nothing on the counter is from yesterday.

  • 3:30 amOvens on. Bread first, pastry after.
  • 6 amDoors open, coffee's on.
