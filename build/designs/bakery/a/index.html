How we bake

Every loaf takes about a day and a half, most of it waiting. We mix in the afternoon, let the dough rest overnight in the cooler, and bake it in the dark so it's on the shelf when you walk in.

No mixes, no commercial yeast in the sourdough, and nothing kept from yesterday except the starter.

  1. 2 pm
    Feed and mixThe starter gets fed, and tomorrow's dough is mixed by hand in small batches.
  2. 3–6 pm
    FoldFour sets of folds while the croissant dough is rolled and turned.
  3. Overnight
    Cold proofShaped loaves sit in baskets in the cooler, twelve to fourteen hours.
  4. 3:30 am
    Ovens onLoaves first, then pastry, then the cardamom buns while the oven drops.
  5. 6 am
    Doors openWhatever's on the rack is on the board.
  6. About 1 pm
    Sold outMost days. Call ahead and we'll hold a loaf with your name on it.
