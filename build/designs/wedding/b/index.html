02

Getting here

Riverside Estate is on the edge of Norman, Oklahoma, about twenty miles south of Oklahoma City.

By air

Will Rogers World Airport

OKC → Norman · 20 miles · about 30 min

The nearest airport, with direct flights from most big US hubs. Rental cars are on site; rideshares pick up outside arrivals.

By car

Down I-35

Oklahoma City → Norman · about 25 min

Take I-35 south and follow the signs for the estate from the exit. Get directions

Parking

Free, on the field by the gate

Someone will wave you in. Cars can stay overnight and be collected by noon on Tuesday, so nobody has to drive after the party.

03

Where to stay

We haven’t reserved a block of rooms, so book early: June weekends fill up around the university.

Near Campus Corner

About 10 min to the estate

A handful of hotels within walking distance of Campus Corner’s restaurants and bars. Best if you want dinner out on Sunday night.

Off I-35, north Norman

About 15 min to the estate

Several chain hotels clustered by the highway, with easy parking and the quickest run back to the airport on Tuesday.

Getting back at night

From 11 pm

We’ll have local taxi numbers at the barn door, and rideshares reach the estate gate.

05

Questions

If yours isn’t here, just ask.

What’s the dress code?

Garden formal. The ceremony is on grass, so flats or block heels are wise, and June in Oklahoma is warm: think light fabrics.

Are kids invited?

It’s an adults-only celebration, apart from the children in the wedding party. Your invitation names everyone we’ve saved a seat for.

Where do I park?

Free parking on the field by the estate gate. Leave the car overnight if you need to and collect it by noon Tuesday.

What if it rains?

The ceremony moves into the barn. We’ll text everyone by noon on the day.

Is there a registry?

Having you there is plenty. If you’d like to give a gift, Our registry has a few ideas.

Can I take photos?

Please keep phones away during the ceremony. After that, snap as much as you like.
