Our story

It started with a bag of the wrong tomatoes and a Saturday market that neither of us has missed since.

2021 · The market

How we met

Jordan had just started volunteering on the produce stall. Ana was the regular who turned up before it opened. He sold her the wrong tomatoes, and she came back the next Saturday to tell him so. She kept coming back.

2026 · The corner

The question

Five summers of Saturdays later, Jordan asked on the same corner of the market, with the right tomatoes this time. Ana said yes before he’d finished the sentence.

Travel & stay

Getting here

Flying

Will Rogers World Airport, Oklahoma City

The closest airport, about 30 minutes north of the estate on I-35. Rental cars and rideshares at arrivals.

Staying

Near Campus Corner, or off I-35

Hotels around Campus Corner are walkable to restaurants and ten minutes from the estate. There’s a cluster of chain hotels off I-35 in north Norman too. We haven’t held a room block, so book early.

Parking

Free, by the gate

Park on the field at Riverside Estate. Cars can stay overnight and be collected by noon the next day. Get directions

Questions

Good to know

What should I wear?

Garden formal. The ceremony is on the lawn, so flats or block heels will keep you from sinking, and June evenings are warm.

Can I bring my kids?

We’re keeping it to adults, apart from the children in our wedding party. Your invitation names everyone we’ve saved a seat for.

Where do I park?

Free on the field by the estate gate. Leave the car overnight if you need to and collect it by noon the next day.

What if it rains?

The ceremony moves into the barn and we’ll let everyone know by noon.
