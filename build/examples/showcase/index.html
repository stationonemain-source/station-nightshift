Since 2009 · Main Street

A family kitchen, the way my grandmother ran hers.

We press tortillas by hand all day, grind our salsas in the molcajete and make the mole from Doña Rosa’s recipe, the same one she cooked in Tlacolula, Oaxaca.

Come for lunch, stay for a mezcal. Kids are welcome, and so is the whole family on Sunday.

The bar · mezcal from Oaxaca

Mezcal from small family palenques.

Our daughter Ana buys from the families who make it: agave roasted in an earth oven, crushed under a stone tahona and distilled in small batches. We pour it in a copita with orange and sal de gusano, the way it is drunk at home.
