Parking

Free street parking on Main Street and the side streets. Evenings and weekends are easiest.

Tables and groups

Walk-ins welcome. For six or more, call ahead and we will hold a table. Bigger than twenty? Use the back room.

Kitchen hours

Last orders go in 30 minutes before close. Pozole is weekends only, until it runs out.

The bar

The bar serves until midnight on Fridays and Saturdays, with a short late menu after ten.

Good to know

  • Step-free entrance

    From Main Street, with an accessible restroom inside.

  • High chairs and a kids’ menu

    Ask your server; crayons are on us.

  • To go

    Call in your order and pick it up at the bar.

  • Gift cards

    Sold at the register in any amount.
