Travel & stay

Norman is twenty minutes south of Oklahoma City.

Flying in

Fly into Will Rogers World Airport (OKC). It’s about a 30 minute drive to the estate down I-35. Rental cars and rideshares are easy to find at arrivals.

Where to stay

Hotels near Campus Corner put you close to restaurants and a short drive from the estate. If you’d rather be near the highway, there are several hotels off I-35 at the north end of town. We haven’t held a block, so book early: June is busy in Norman.

Getting there & parking

Free parking on the field by the gate, with someone to wave you in. If you plan to celebrate properly, leave the car and take a taxi home. Get directions

Good to know

The questions we’ve been asked most.

What should I wear?

Garden formal. The ceremony is on grass, so block heels or flats will save you sinking. It can still be warm at six; bring a light layer for later.

Can I bring the kids?

We love them, but this is an adults-only evening, apart from the children in our wedding party. Your invitation lists everyone we’ve saved a seat for.

Where do I park?

On the field by the estate gate, free all day. Cars can stay overnight and be collected by noon the next day.
