From the baker

Twelve loaves on a borrowed oven, and then a shop.

Sam Whitley, baker and owner

“If the bread isn't right at four in the morning, it doesn't go on the shelf. That's the whole rule.”

I started baking for the Saturday market, twelve loaves at a time in a friend's restaurant oven after they closed. People kept asking where they could get it on a Tuesday. Eventually the answer was this room on East Main.
