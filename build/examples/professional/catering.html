How it works

From request to pickup.

Prefer to talk it through? Call (405) 555-0142 between 6 am and 1 pm.

  1. You send a request

    The form above, an email to orders@hearthstreetbakery.com, or a call.

  2. We confirm the price

    Same day, by email. Pay when you pick up, or by card over the phone for orders over $150.

  3. You pick it up

    Boxed and labelled with your name, at the counter at the time you chose.
