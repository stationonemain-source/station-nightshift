Getting here

On East Main, a short walk from downtown.

Look for the bread in the window. If the lights are on and the door's propped open, we've still got some.

Parking

Free street parking along Main and the side streets. Two-hour limit until 6 pm.

Getting in

One step-free entrance on Main Street. The counter and the window tables are wheelchair accessible.

Sitting down

Three tables by the window and a bench outside when it's warm. Bikes go on the rack by the door.

Before you come

Things people ask at the counter.

Can I reserve a loaf?

Yes. Call before 10 am on the day and we'll put it behind the counter with your name on it until we close.

Do you take cards?

Cards, phones and cash. No minimum.

Is anything gluten-free?

No. Everything is made in one small kitchen with wheat flour in the air, so we can't promise anything is safe for coeliacs.

Can I bring my dog?

Dogs are welcome on the bench outside, and there's a water bowl by the door.

Do you have Wi-Fi?

We do. Ask for the password at the counter.
