How our pricing works

Labor is the starting price you see on the list. Bigger or awkward jobs cost more, and you'll see why on the quote.

Parts are charged at what we pay plus a fair handling margin, itemized on your quote before we start.

No trip charge inside Norman, Moore and Noble. Outside them, we'll tell you the charge on the phone.

Payment when the job is done and you've seen it: card, check or cash.

How a job goes

Three steps. You always know the price before we pick up a tool.

  1. Call or send the formTell us what's wrong. A photo from your phone helps.
  2. We come and lookUsually within two working days. Written quote, free.
  3. We fix itOn the day we agreed. We clean up and walk you through it.

If something we fixed fails within a year, we come back and put it right at no charge. Licensed and insured in Oklahoma.
