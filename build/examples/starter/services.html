Before you book

The questions we get asked most at the front desk.

How long will colour take?

A root touch-up is about 90 minutes. Highlights take two to two and a half hours, and balayage around three. We will give you a time when we confirm.

Why do colour prices say “from”?

Long or very thick hair uses more colour and more time. We quote at your consultation, before anything is mixed, and the price does not change on the day.

Should I come with clean hair?

Come as you are. For colour, hair washed a day or two before is ideal. For curly cuts, come with your curls dry and styled the way you usually wear them.

Can I bring my children?

Yes. Children’s cuts are $24 for under-12s, and Tuesday walk-ins are a good time to bring them.
