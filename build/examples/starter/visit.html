Your first visit

  1. We confirm by phone or text.

    Usually the same day. If you asked for colour, we book a quick consultation and patch test first.

  2. Come in and sit down.

    Coffee or tea while we talk through what you want. Bring photos if you have them.

  3. Wash, then the chair.

    Your stylist tells you the price before starting, and checks in before anything big is cut or coloured.

  4. Pay at the desk.

    Cash, card or tap. Book your next visit there, or just call when you are ready.
