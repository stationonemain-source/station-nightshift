Three chairs, three stylists

Book with whoever suits your hair. If you are not sure, tell us what you want when you request a time and we will match you.

Owner · colour

Maren

Balayage, blonding and colour corrections. Fourteen years behind the chair, the last eight on Gray Street.

Cuts · curls · blowouts

Jess

Precision cuts, curly and wavy hair, and the best blowout in Norman, if you ask her regulars.

Men’s cuts · barbering

Theo

Scissor cuts, fades, beard shaping and straight-razor shaves. Quiet chair, quick hands.

A small studio, on purpose

Three chairs, one wash basin and a lot of plants. The music stays low, the coffee is free, and we book enough time that nobody is rushed out of the chair.

We use ammonia-free colour where we can and will talk you through anything we put on your hair.
