Is asking for reviews allowed?

Asking is fine everywhere. Buying isn't, gating isn't — and we do neither. Every ask is to a real customer after a real job.

What about a bad review?

It's caught the minute it lands, you're alerted, and a calm draft is waiting — approval-only for low stars, always.

Will replies sound like a bot?

They're drafted from your tone setting and the review's actual content. Most owners post them untouched; you can edit every one.
