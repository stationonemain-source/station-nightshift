Frequently asked questions

Is this a lead-generation service?

No. We don't sell shared lists or contact information. We introduce one contractor who has a real job to one crew that wants it, and the relationship is theirs from there.

Who pays?

Only the crew that receives a job, and only after their first free job: $250 per job to quote, or $497 per month for a network membership. Sending work is always free.

Do you contact homeowners?

Never. BidBridge works only with contractors.

How do you text me?

Only if you join and check a texting box on our join form. Texts come from (405) 814-0793. Reply HELP for help or STOP to opt out at any time.

Where do you operate?

We started in the Oklahoma City metro and are adding more areas. Join and tell us your city.

What if a crew doesn't deliver?

Network members get at least 2 real jobs to quote in their first 20 introductions, or we replace the partners who didn't deliver.

How do I cancel?

Membership is month to month. Email main@station.solutions and we'll cancel it, no questions asked.
