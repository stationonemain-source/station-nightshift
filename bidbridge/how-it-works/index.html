How it works

One contractor has a job outside their trade. Another contractor wants that kind of work. We put them together, fast.

Step by step

  1. You join. Fill out the join form with your trade and city, and choose whether we may text you.
  2. A job comes in. A contractor in our network tells us about a job that needs another trade's bid: the town, the scope and the timing.
  3. We check it. We confirm the details with them by phone and get their permission to share it with one crew.
  4. We offer it to a crew. A vetted crew in that trade near the job gets the offer. If they want it, we introduce the two contractors.
  5. They meet and quote. From there it's their relationship. We check back in a couple of days to make sure the quote happened.

What we never do

We never contact homeowners. We work only with contractors.

We never sell or share your number. Your details go only to the one contractor you agree to be introduced to.

We never touch insurance claims or deductibles. That stays between the contractor and the homeowner.
