About BidBridge

Most home jobs need more than one trade. The roofer doesn't do siding, the builder doesn't paint, and the homeowner shouldn't have to hunt for the next crew. BidBridge makes that handoff easy.

Who we are

BidBridge is a service of Station Automations Group LLC, a software and services company that builds tools for local businesses. We started BidBridge in the Oklahoma City metro, where hail season means a lot of roofs and a lot of siding, gutters and paint that need a second crew.

What we believe

Relationships, not shared lists

Each job goes to one crew, not ten. Contractors build real partnerships that keep sending work both ways.

Real jobs only

Every job offer is a real, confirmed job from a contractor who asked us to find a crew.

Respect your time and your phone

We only text contractors who join and say yes, and STOP always works.
