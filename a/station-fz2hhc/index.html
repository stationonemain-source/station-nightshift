What we found

7 gaps, most important first
Gap

After-hours lead capture

What we saw: Claims 24/7 but has no chat/answering system: an unanswered call breaks the promise.

A call after hours reaches voicemail or nothing; the enquiry is not captured.

Quick fix: Make your site and voicemail capture a name and number after hours, and call back first thing.

Gap

Self-serve booking

What we saw: No self-serve booking path: calling is the only way in.

There is no way to book or request a slot without calling during hours.

Quick fix: Put a 'Book now' button on your homepage that links to a booking page, even a free one.

Gap

Instant answers on your site

What we saw: No chat widget on the site.

A visitor with a quick question has no way to ask it without picking up the phone.

Quick fix: Add a chat or 'ask a question' button to every page, and answer within minutes during business hours.

Gap

Your Google listing

What we saw: Google listing: only 0 photos, no hours listed.

Your Google listing is missing details customers look for before they call.

Quick fix: Fill in every field on your Google Business Profile: hours, services, photos and a description.

Gap

Lead follow-up

What we saw: A contact form exists with no follow-up system behind it.

Enquiries that do not buy on the spot are never followed up.

Quick fix: Follow up every enquiry that doesn't book within 24 hours, then again three days later.

Gap

Your customer list

What we saw: No email-marketing footprint: the customer list never hears from them.

Past customers never hear from you; there is no email list at work.

Quick fix: Start collecting customer emails and send one genuinely useful note a month.

Gap

Being seen

What we saw: Social links exist but no ad pixel: posting maybe, measuring nothing: verify their feed by hand.

Your social presence has gone quiet, so the site is the only thing selling.

Quick fix: Post one real photo of your work each week.
