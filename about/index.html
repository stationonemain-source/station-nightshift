Why Station exists.

The businesses that run a town lose most of their work in small, fixable places: a call nobody picked up, a quote nobody chased, a happy customer nobody asked for a review.

Station is the set of tools that closes those gaps, each one priced on its own page, live in days, and month to month. When nothing on the shelf fits, we build the thing that does.

How we work.

  • We’ll tell you when you don’t need it.Every product page says who it isn’t for, and the calculator says so when the math is thin. A customer who bought the wrong thing is worse for us than no customer.
  • You see it before you pay.The audit is free. A website demo is free. Every product and collection starts with 14 days free, and $0 is charged today.
  • Nothing you can’t leave.Month to month, cancel from your account. Your number, your domain, your Google profile and your brand board stay yours when you go.
  • The fine print, out loud.Prices, allowances and what happens when you go over are written on every page, before you buy.
  • You talk to the people who build it.There are two of us and no account managers. Write in and one of us reads it the same day.
