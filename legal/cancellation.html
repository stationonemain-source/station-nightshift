Station — Cancellation & Refund Policy

Station Automations Group LLC · 1856 Branard St, Houston, TX 77098 Service: Station AI (my.station.solutions) · Effective: October 13, 2026 · Updated: November 4, 2026

The short version

  • No long-term contracts. Every plan is month-to-month.
  • 14 days free. Your 14 days free start the day your first product goes live, not today. Nothing is charged while we set you up, and you can cancel any time from Billing.
  • Cancel any time from Billing. Your products run until the end of the paid month.
  • No refunds of a month already charged, unless the law requires one.
  • You leave with your data. A download of everything in your account, and your number stays yours for 30 days to move it.
  • Your domain is yours. The website that came with your plan comes down when the plan ends, unless you keep it for a one-time $1,250.

1. Plans this covers

Every Station plan, Collection and individual product. Each is a monthly subscription. Current prices are on station.solutions and on each product page; this policy does not repeat them, so it never disagrees with them.

2. How to cancel

  • Use the Billing menu in your dashboard at my.station.solutions, or email main@station.solutions from the account owner's email address.
  • When it takes effect: your products run until the end of the paid month. We don't prorate partial months.
  • Cancelling from Billing shows “Cancelled at the end of the paid month.” on screen. If you email us, we confirm by reply within 1 business day; if you don't receive that reply, your request didn't reach us — please contact us again.
  • During the 14 days free nothing has been charged, so cancelling then costs nothing. If your account is still in set-up 90 days after you sign up, the free period ends and monthly billing starts; finish set-up and go live, or cancel from Billing.

3. Refunds

  • Months already charged: no refund of a month already charged, unless the law requires one.
  • Website build fees: not refunded once build work has begun. If you cancel before we've started any build work, the fee is refunded in full.
  • Billing errors: if we ever bill you incorrectly, tell us and we'll correct it and refund the difference promptly.

4. Offboarding — what you get when you leave

When your subscription ends we email you a download of everything we hold for you (contacts, conversations, call records, bookings, reviews, campaigns and reports). The link works for 30 days. After that your account and its data are deleted. Your number stays yours for 30 days to move it.

The website that comes with your plan

  • A website Station builds as part of a plan or Collection stays live while you are subscribed.
  • When the plan ends, it comes down within 72 hours of the end of your paid period, unless you buy it out for a one-time $1,250. We email you the offer when you cancel, with a link to pay.
  • If you buy it out, it stays online with no gap: we transfer the website's code to your own GitHub account and its domain to you.
  • If you don't, the domain is still yours: we email you its transfer code when the site comes down, with the records to point it somewhere new.
  • A website you paid a setup fee for is already yours; when its hosting & care ends we send you its files and its domain.

5. Your phone number

Your business phone number is yours. Number porting is supported into Station when you join, and your number stays yours for 30 days after your subscription ends to move it to any provider.

6. Chargebacks

Please contact us before filing a chargeback with your bank. We resolve legitimate billing errors promptly — almost always faster than the card-dispute process. Chargebacks filed on correctly delivered service may result in account suspension while we resolve the dispute.

Questions

Email main@station.solutions — we answer billing questions within 1 business day.
