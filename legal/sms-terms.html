1. The program

Program name: Station AI business messaging — covering both Station's own customer care and marketing messages and messages sent on behalf of the business you contacted.

Station AI powers text messaging for local businesses. When you text with a business that uses Station AI, the messages come from that specific business — Station provides the technology that sends and receives them on the business's behalf.

Station also messages people who contact Station directly — for example, when you request a free audit on our website. Station does not currently operate a text-messaging number of its own and replies to direct enquiries by email. If that changes, this page will name the number before any message is sent from it.

2. What messages you'll receive

Three separate programs run under these terms. Which one applies depends on who you gave your number to.

A. Messages from Station itself — if you contacted Station (for example, by requesting a free audit at station.solutions/audit/), Station currently replies by email only. If Station texts again, messages will come from a number named on this page and may include:

  • A text-back when we miss your call, and replies to questions you send us;
  • Your requested audit results and the link to your report;
  • Confirmations and reminders for calls you book with us;
  • Occasional promotional and marketing messages — tips, offers, and updates about Station's services.

B. Messages from a business that uses Station AI — if you contacted one of our client businesses, messages come from that business and may include:

  • Appointment confirmations and reminders;
  • Replies to inquiries you sent to that business (by text, web chat, or form);
  • Review requests after a visit or service;
  • Service updates from that business.

Message frequency varies depending on your interaction with Station or the business.

C. BidBridge (a Station service for contractors) — BidBridge connects home-service contractors in Oklahoma with each other for job referrals. Messages come from BidBridge's Oklahoma number and may include:

  • A first message to a contractor's publicly listed business number, asking whether the business has jobs that need another trade's bid, or offering a specific job to quote. Every first message names BidBridge and explains how to opt out;
  • Replies to questions you send us, and introductions to another contractor you agreed to meet;
  • Follow-ups about a referral (for example, "did you get to quote the job?");
  • Offers of further job referrals and of BidBridge's paid referral services.

BidBridge texts businesses about business referrals only, never homeowners or consumers. We send between 9 AM and 7 PM Central, and we will not send a second unanswered message to a number that never replied. Numbers that reply STOP are never texted again.

3. How you opt in

For Station's own messages (program A): you opt in by entering your phone number on a Station web form and ticking the SMS consent checkbox, which is unchecked by default and separate from the email consent checkbox. The checkbox states that you will receive messages about your audit and your account as well as occasional Station tips and offers. Submitting the form without ticking that box gets you the audit by email and no text messages.

For a client business's messages (program B): you opt in by providing your phone number to that business through its own forms, booking flows, or chat — for example, when you book an appointment, fill out a contact form, or start a text conversation with that business. Your consent is to receive messages from that specific business, not from other businesses.

For BidBridge (program C): you can join by texting our number, by submitting the form at station.solutions/bidbridge/ with its SMS checkbox ticked (unchecked by default), or by agreeing on a phone call. BidBridge may also send one first message to a business phone number that the business publishes (for example on its website, its Google listing or a state contractor registry); that message identifies BidBridge, and if you do not reply we will not message again. Reply STOP at any time and we will never text you again.

Consent is not a condition of purchase. Under the TCPA, agreeing to receive text messages is never required to buy any product or service from the business.

4. Costs

Message and data rates may apply, according to your mobile carrier plan. Station and the businesses using Station AI do not charge you for receiving messages.

5. Opting out — STOP

Reply STOP to any message to opt out. Opt-out is immediate and honored platform-wide — you will receive one final confirmation of your opt-out and nothing further from that number. You can re-subscribe at any time by replying START or by opting in again through the business.

6. Getting help — HELP

Reply HELP to any message for assistance, or email main@station.solutions. For questions about a specific conversation, appointment, or service, contact the business you were messaging with directly.

7. Carriers

Mobile carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. Message delivery is subject to carrier network availability.

8. Your privacy

Phone numbers and message content are processed on behalf of the business you contacted, as described in our Privacy Policy. We keep records of opt-ins and opt-outs as proof of consent. We never sell personal data.
