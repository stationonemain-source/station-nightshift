4. How the opt-in works

  • The SMS checkbox is unchecked by default. Consent is affirmative and express.
  • It is a separate checkbox from the email consent checkbox. Ticking one does not tick the other.
  • It is not required to submit the form. A visitor who leaves it unticked still receives the requested audit by email and receives no text messages.
  • Consent is not a condition of purchase, and this is stated in the checkbox itself.
  • The checkbox sits directly below the phone number field, so the number being consented for is visible at the moment of consent.
  • The consent state, timestamp, and the page URL are stored with the submission as the record of opt-in.

6. Frequency, cost, help and opt-out

  • Frequency: varies by interaction; stated in the checkbox as "Message frequency varies."
  • Cost: "Msg & data rates may apply" is stated in the checkbox. Station does not charge for messages.
  • HELP: replying HELP returns support contact details; also stated in the checkbox.
  • STOP: replying STOP opts the consumer out immediately, with one final confirmation and nothing after that. Opt-outs are honored platform-wide.
  • All of the above are repeated in full in the SMS Terms & Consent Policy, which is linked from the checkbox itself.

7. What Station does not do

  • No purchased, rented, or third-party lists. Every number is typed in by its owner on the form above.
  • No affiliate marketing, and no messages on behalf of a third party from this number.
  • Consent is never shared with or sold to anyone else.
